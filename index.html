About

Who you'd be hiring

I'm in my final years of a Computer Science degree at the American University of Technology, and most of what I know I learned by shipping something. I started with course projects, kept going after the grade was in, and ended up with apps that have real users in mind: onboarding, empty states, settings, edge cases, the parts that usually get skipped.

My strongest work is on Android in Kotlin and on the web in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with C# and .NET for desktop and PHP with MySQL when a project needs a backend. I design the interface before I build it, and I'm comfortable being the person who redoes a layout three times until it stops looking like a student project.

Outside of code I'm an active member of the IEEE Student Branch and a confident public speaker — I like presenting work as much as building it.

SudoMind

Android Sudoku app · Kotlin, Android Studio

A full Sudoku app built end to end, with a hint engine that explains moves using real solving techniques instead of revealing an answer from the solution grid. Around it sits everything a published app needs: daily challenges, streaks, in-app events, statistics, themes and a settings system.

  • Custom hint engine based on human solving techniques, not brute-force lookup
  • Swipeable difficulty tabs, animated how-to-play tutorial and custom win dialogs
  • Live event countdowns with persistent notifications
  • In release preparation for Google Play, including AdMob integration
